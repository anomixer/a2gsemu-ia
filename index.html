    ℹ️ About Apple IIgs

    Apple IIgs is a 16-bit personal computer released by Apple Computer in September 1986, the last product in the Apple II series.

    Main Specifications:

    • 2.8 MHz 65C816 processor
    • 256 KB RAM (expandable to 8 MB)
    • Support for 4096 color display
    • Ensoniq 5503 sound chip (32 channels)
    • Backward compatible with Apple II series software
